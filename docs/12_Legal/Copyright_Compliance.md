# 1. Purpose and Scope

This Copyright Compliance document defines the trademark disclaimers, fair use justifications, intellectual property policies, and content attribution guidelines for the Pokémon Knowledge Management Platform (PKMP) v1.0.0. The platform is operated strictly as a non-commercial, educational fan project.

---

# 2. Trademark Disclaimer & Fair Use

- **Trademark Ownership:** All Pokémon names, logos, characters, sprites, move definitions, stats, and related media are owned by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., and The Pokémon Company.
- **Project Classification:** PKMP is a fan-created platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with the trademark holders.
- **Fair Use Justification:** Under US Copyright Law (17 U.S. Code § 107), the platform's usage of franchise assets is justified as Fair Use based on:
  1. *Purpose:* Non-commercial, educational, and analytical.
  2. *Nature:* Compilation of public database statistics.
  3. *Market Effect:* The platform does not compete with official games or merchandise, and does not charge subscription fees or show advertisements.

---

# 3. Content Attribution Policies

To prevent copyright issues, the platform enforces these content policies:

- **Official Data Labeling:** All pages displaying official assets must display the copyright disclaimer footer (REQ-LEG-100).
- **Community Submissions (Fakemon):** Any community-submitted artwork or content must be uploaded with explicit licenses (Creative Commons CC-BY-NC or public domain) and labeled as "Community Content" to prevent confusion with official trademarked assets.
