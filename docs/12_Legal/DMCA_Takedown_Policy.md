# 1. Purpose and Scope

This DMCA Takedown Policy document defines the notification procedures, agent contacts, verification checkpoints, and content removal timelines for resolving copyright infringement claims on the Pokémon Knowledge Management Platform (PKMP) v1.0.0.

---

# 2. Designated Copyright Agent

Trademark holders and copyright owners can submit copyright infringement notices to:

- **Email Contact:** `dmca@pkmp.org`
- **Mailing Address:** TBD (Assigned in Production configuration)
- **Response SLA:** DMCA tickets are reviewed and processed within 48 hours.

---

# 3. Filing a Takedown Notice

To submit a valid DMCA request, notices must be sent in writing and include:

1. **Identification of Work:** The copyright-protected work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., link to official source).
2. **Identification of Infringing Content:** The location (URL or database identifier) of the infringing asset on the platform.
3. **Contact Information:** Full name, email, phone number, and physical address.
4. **Good Faith Statement:** A statement that the copyright holder has a good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
5. **Accuracy Statement:** A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notice is accurate.
6. **Signature:** Electronic or physical signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.

---

# 4. Content Removal and Counter-Notices

- **Immediate Takedown:** Upon receiving a valid notice, the system administrator will immediately disable access to the infringing material.
- **Counter-Notice:** If a contributor believes their community-submitted asset was removed in error, they can file a counter-notice containing:
  - Identification of the removed material.
  - A statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was a result of mistake or misidentification.
  - Consent to local jurisdiction.
- **Reinstatement:** If no legal action is filed by the copyright claimant within 10 business days of receiving the counter-notice, the material may be restored.
